02 · PROBLEM

Risks form at the orchard, but only surface at the border.

Cadmium contamination and code fraud are the visible symptoms; the root causes lie in broken procurement chains, fragmented data, and disconnected quality controls.

Unscreened inputs

Soil, water, fertilizers and pesticides can introduce Cadmium risks; premature harvest or chemical treatment degrades shipment quality.

Unreliable shipment identity

Mixed-source collection, misuse or lending of PUC/PHC codes means documentation no longer reflects the actual growing area and packing house.

Controls stacked at the end of the chain

When China inspects at the border, all accumulated errors surface simultaneously — too late to fix for fresh produce.

Estimated financial exposure
2–4 billionVND / container
+ Demurrage and cold-chain fees+ Fire sale or destruction of goods+ PUC/PHC suspension and lost orders
